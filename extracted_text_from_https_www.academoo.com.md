## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Academoo?

Academoo is an interactive learning games platform built for teachers, speech-language pathologists, and parents. Create custom question sets and use them with a growing collection of engaging classroom games.

Who is Academoo for?

Teachers, speech-language pathologists (SLPs), and parents who want to make learning fun and interactive.

Is Academoo free?

Yes. You can create question sets, pick from our library, and play games for free.

What subjects are covered?

Math, spelling, vocabulary, reading, and any subject you want. You can also create custom question sets with your own content.

Can I create my own games?

Absolutely! Create question sets with text, images, and audio, then use them with any of our games.

Do I need to install anything?

No! Academoo runs in your browser on desktop, tablet, and phone. No downloads or installations needed.

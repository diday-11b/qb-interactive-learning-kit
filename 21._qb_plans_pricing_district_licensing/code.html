Frequently Asked Questions

Everything school boards, principals, and teachers need to know before joining.

lock How does student privacy work? (Zero ad tracking, zero student emails needed) expand_more
QB Interactive Learning Kits operates on a 100% ad-free, zero-tracker architecture. Elementary students never need an email address; they join via a secure 6-character game code or Clever badge. We comply fully with FERPA, COPPA, and CSPC guidelines, and we never sell student data or use inputs for external LLM training.
receipt_long Can we pay via School Purchase Order (PO) or Title I / ESSER Grants? expand_more
Yes! We accept official district Purchase Orders (Net-30 or Net-60 terms), credit cards, checks, and ACH wire transfers. QB Interactive Learning Kits qualifies as evidence-based supplementary learning software under Title I (School Improvement) and Title III (English Learners) grant funding.
home_app_logo Can parents use this at home with homework codes? expand_more
Absolutely. When a teacher publishes an assignment or articulation practice deck, students can log in from home on any iPad, Chromebook, or home computer using their single-click student card. Parents receive weekly email badges celebrating mastered spelling, math facts, and reading stamina.
request_quote Can I get reimbursed with my classroom stipend? expand_more
Yes. Every Pro invoice generates an itemized, PDF receipt compliant with typical classroom supply stipends and PTA mini-grants. If your district later purchases an enterprise license, we will prorate and refund your individual subscription.

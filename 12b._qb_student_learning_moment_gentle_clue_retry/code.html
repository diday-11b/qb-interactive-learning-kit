Leo's 2-Step Wonder Method:

01
STEP 1: Find Matching Slices

1/2 is equal to 2/4!

Multiply top and bottom by 2 so both fractions share fourths (4ths).

1/2 ➔ 2/4
02
STEP 2: Add The Tops!

3/4 + 2/4 = 5/4

Keep the bottom the same (4), and add the tops: 3 + 2 = 5! (Which is 1 and 1/4 whole pizzas!)
